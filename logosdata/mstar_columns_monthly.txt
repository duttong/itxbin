# site         Station code (see above)
# yyyy         Year of the monthly mean (UTC)
# mm           Month of the monthly mean, 1-12 (UTC)
# dec_date     Mid-month decimal year (UTC)
# {param}     Dry-air mole fraction (pmol/mol, ppt) — mean of the flask pair
#              means collected at this site during the month
# {param}_sd  1 standard deviation of the flask pair means in the month
# {param}_n   Number of flask pairs contributing to the monthly mean
#
# Months run continuously from each site's first to its last sampled month.
# A month with no accepted flask pair is reported as nan with n = 0, and a
# month containing a single flask pair has no measurable spread, so its
# standard deviation is also reported as nan.
#
# Flask pairs with a statistically discernible difference at the 90–95%
# confidence level are excluded.  Additional culling is applied based on
# relative within-pair differences.  Results are from simultaneously filled
# stainless steel or glass flask pairs.
