# site         Station code (see above)
# yyyy         Year of the monthly mean (UTC)
# mm           Month of the monthly mean, 1-12 (UTC)
# dec_date     Mid-month decimal year (UTC)
# {param}     Dry-air mole fraction (pmol/mol, ppt) — mean of the flask pair
#              means collected at this site during the month
# {param}_sd  1 standard deviation of the flask pair means in the month,
#              subject to the floor described below
# {param}_n   Number of flask pairs contributing to the monthly mean
#
# Months run continuously from each site's first to its last sampled month.
# A month with no accepted flask pair is reported as nan with n = 0.
#
# STANDARD DEVIATION FLOOR
#
# A month built from one or two flask pairs reports a standard deviation near
# zero whenever those pairs happen to agree.  That is an artefact of the sample
# size, not a statement that the month was tightly constrained, so such months
# are floored at the recent typical scatter for the same site: the mean of the
# usable monthly standard deviations over the trailing 12 months, being the
# month itself and the 11 before it.  Where the measured standard deviation
# already exceeds that floor it is reported unchanged.
#
# Only months of one or two flask pairs are floored.  A month of three or more
# measures its own spread well enough to keep it, and flooring it would both
# discard that information and bias the record upward, since a floor raises a
# value and never lowers one.
#
# Averaging the existing monthly spreads, rather than recomputing a standard
# deviation across the 12-month window, keeps the floor independent of the
# trend.  A gas moving several ppt per year would otherwise be given a floor
# that reflects its rise rather than the precision of the measurement.
#
# Months holding a single flask pair have no spread of their own.  They are
# left out of the window average, so their zero cannot drag the floor down, but
# they are assigned the floor as their standard deviation -- it is the only one
# available for them.  Where the trailing window rests on fewer than three
# usable months no floor is formed: the measured standard deviation is reported
# as it stands, or as nan for a single-pair month.
#
# Flask pairs with a statistically discernible difference at the 90–95%
# confidence level are excluded.  Additional culling is applied based on
# relative within-pair differences.  Results are from simultaneously filled
# stainless steel or glass flask pairs.
